## Convolver 0.14.0 failed the Audacity walkthrough at A6

With Convolver's window open, Audacity exported the original, unfiltered
audio. After I closed the window and exported again, Audacity produced
silence.

I tested the checksum-verified release in **Audacity 4.0.2.263060743** on
**macOS 27.2.1 (26B131), Apple silicon**. Audacity's default sample rate was
already **48,000 Hz**. Both exports were full-project, stereo, 48,000 Hz,
32-bit float WAVs. Core Audio, buffer 100 ms; the plug-in logged
Accelerate/vDSP, 2 inputs, 2 outputs, and blocks of up to 8,192 frames.

| Step | Observation |
|---|---|
| **A4 · Choose the filter** | Convolver showed **"None"** and **"Load failed."** It refused the 48,000 Hz filter because it expected 44,100 Hz. The same window said the host had yet to send its audio settings. |
| **A5 · Inspect Undo** | The Edit menu said **"Undo 'Add Convolver'."** |
| **A6 · Export with the window open** | **Fail.** The export differed from the expected filtered audio by up to **0.876**. Compared with the dry input, the largest sample difference was **zero**. The export log said no filter was loaded and audio passed unchanged. (`a1.wav`) |
| **D1 · Close the window and export again** | **Fail.** The export was silent. The log said Audacity restored a state recording that the filter had failed to load. (`d1.wav`) |

**What this establishes.** The first failure occurred when choosing the
filter, before export. The editor rejected it against 44,100 Hz, although
both export instances subsequently started at 48,000 Hz. Closing the editor
made Audacity retain the *failed-load state*. Whether Audacity retains a
**successfully loaded** filter remains unverified.

I repeated the choice in a fresh Audacity session after a preliminary
window-focus issue. The same refusal recurred. During the recorded A6 run,
playback stayed stopped, the editor stayed open, and I made no plug-in
control edits. I stopped after D1 as the walkthrough directs, so
save/reopen, Recent, Undo after a second choice, and the optional 0.13.2
control were not run. **0.14.0 remains installed.**
