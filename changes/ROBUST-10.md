# ROBUST-10

## Fixed

- Command line (ROBUST-10): with `--gain-db no-clip` and float output, a
  sample that the convolution's float rounding takes a hair over full scale
  (1.0000001 for full-scale DC through a filter of 9,600 taps of 1.0) is no
  longer reported as input beyond full scale. The note says it is rounding
  and that 0.001 dB more attenuation keeps it within full scale. See
  [rendering](../docs/use/command-line.md#file-rendering).

## For contributors

- Tests (ROBUST-10): `native.torture` renders filters that real IRs rarely
  reach, generated by the test rather than kept as files: a 60 s noise tail
  checked frame by frame, a unit impulse padded to 1,048,577 taps that must
  pass audio bit for bit, pure delays up to 10 s, full-scale DC through a
  filter of 9,600 ones, a tail decaying through the subnormal range, rates
  from 1 Hz to 768 kHz and 256 channels, with the refusals just beyond.
  `torture_tests.py --write-irs DIR` writes the filters for listening or
  host tests. See [native tests](../docs/develop/testing.md#native-production-tests).
