# IO-22

## Fixed

- C++ library (IO-22): a `Quantizer` moved from could write past its storage
  on the next `quantize()`, as a defaulted move emptied its buffers but kept
  its channel count. It now copies instead of moving, so the source stays
  usable. `io.hpp` also says that the quantizer ignores
  `QuantizationOptions::valid_bits`
  ([package guide](../docs/develop/library.md#writing-files)).

## For contributors

- Tests (IO-22): `native.io` quantizes with a moved-from and a move-assigned
  `Quantizer`. From the second audit of 0.22.0 (§1.3).
